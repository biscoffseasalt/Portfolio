Capstone — AEE25-010

ASTRA Kit

A low-cost, modular 1U CubeSat training platform built from COTS components and interconnected through a custom backplane board. ASTRA Kit integrates five spacecraft subsystems (Structural, EPS, C&DHS, Communication, and Payload) with a hybrid LoRa/WiFi communication architecture and web-based ground station for real-time monitoring and payload data visualization.

Unit Development Cost: ₱5,876.08
